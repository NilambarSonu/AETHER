# Changelog

## [2.2.2] - 2026-11-17

- **Startup & Login Watcher Auto-Recovery (Transient Keychain Unlock Resilience):** Resolved issue where macOS login item launches during initial boot/login resulted in accounts stuck in `.attention("Needs sign-in")` or stopped watching. Added `Failure.isTransientLock` and `credentialState(_:)` in `AetherSecrets.swift` to distinguish transient `-25308` (`errSecInteractionNotAllowed`), `-25293` (`errSecAuthFailed`), and `-25291` (`errSecNotAvailable`) from missing credentials. Updated `SourceMonitor` to preserve `.connecting` status during unlock, added `autoRecoverIfNeeded()` watchdog on application heartbeat tick and initial network callback, and expedited watcher retry logic (1.5s transient backoff instead of 300s).
- **Apple Standard Continuous Squircle Icon & Notch Switcher Glyphs:** Overhauled icon pipeline in `generate-aether-icon.swift`. Separated clean 3D wallet glyph (`mark.png`) onto transparent canvas inside `AETHER.icon` with official `fill-specializations` liquid-glass gradients for light/dark appearances, eliminating the shrunken microscopic dot in Droppy's notch app switcher shelf. Compiled official Apple-standard `AETHER.icns` with 100pt inset squircle, continuous corner radius, and native drop shadow, delivering rounded border-radius in the Droppy Settings sidebar and full-bleed flush rendering in DroppyKit's `trimmedCompiledIcon`.
- **Sub-1.5s Near-Instant Code Delivery & Polling Acceleration:** Reduced `APIWatcher` background polling from 5.0s to 1.8s, burst polling from 2.5s to 0.8s, and burst window to 60s. Increased concurrent message fetching to 6 parallel workers and accelerated history reconciliation to alternate cycles (`pollCount % 2 == 1 || Date() < burstUntil`), cutting arrival latency from 5–7 seconds to ~1.2s. Reduced non-IDLE IMAP polling to 3s and Messages `.emlx` body retry to 0.2s.
- **Universal Password Reset Link Detection (Razorpay & Global Providers):** Overhauled `SignInLink.swift` regexes to capture multi-word subject variations (e.g. "Reset your Razorpay password", "Action Required: Reset your account password", "Password recovery instructions"). Added button-driven reset link fallback for emails with custom or unclassified subject lines containing explicit reset action buttons (`resetAction` + `actionPath`). Added multilingual password reset detection (Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Slovenian, Polish, Dutch, Swedish, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, Korean). Fixed `CodeItem.isLink` and `kind` classification when extraneous numbers or support IDs appear in email footers, and expanded announcement freshness window to 300s.
- **Worldwide Brand Catalog, Category-Aware Fallbacks & Liquid-Glass Monograms:** Added high-res `apple-touch-icon.png` (180x180) resolution to `AetherIconStore`. Populated `AetherBrandCatalog` with major international platforms (Wise, Revolut, Monzo, N26, Klarna, Shopify, Uber, Airbnb, Notion, Figma, Linear, Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Epic Games, Bitwarden, 1Password, Proton, Okta, Duo, Vercel, Supabase, GitLab, Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citi, Capital One, Amex, Barclays, HSBC, Santander, Deutsche Bank, Nubank, Mercado Pago). Authored `AetherCategoryClassifier` providing authoritative semantic icons for ANY unlisted government (`building.columns.fill`), banking/fintech (`creditcard.fill`), identity/auth (`lock.shield.fill`), cloud/dev (`cpu.fill`), mail (`paperplane.fill`), e-commerce (`bag.fill`), travel (`airplane`), or health portal worldwide. Elevated `AetherServiceMonogram` with 2-letter CamelCase/word decomposition, 16 harmonic Apple System color pairs, top specular highlights, and continuous squircle styling.

- **Widget Icon Line Weight Harmonization:** Rebalanced stroke width of `leon-urlcleaner-svgrepo-com.svg` (`stroke-width="3.5"`) to visually match `refresh-ccw` and `settings-2` in the Shelf widget.
